MORE THAN A MARKETPLACE

A little trading.
A lot of community.

SkillSwap is built around a simple idea: everyone has something valuable to offer. Whether you're trading a haircut for fresh eggs or tutoring for homemade bread, there's no need for every exchange to involve money.

Our goal is to bring neighbors together, encourage resourcefulness, and make everyday exchanges easier.
